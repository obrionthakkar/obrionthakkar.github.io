Richmond is easily accessible from major airports. We recommend flying into Richmond International Airport (RIC), which is about 20 minutes from the venue. Amtrak offers service to Richmond, with a stops at Richmond Main Street (RVM) Station and Richmond Staples Mill Road (RVR) Station, both located within 10 minutes of the venue. Richmond is also accessible by car (approximately 2 hours from Washington, DC) and there is ample parking available at the venue. Ubers & Lyfts are readily accessible throughout the city.


We are over the moon to welcome you to Richmond. This historic city has delightful surprises around every corner, and we hope you take some time to appreciate the people who make it special. A few suggestions...

Visit the Kehinde Wileys at the VMFA, recite “The Raven” at the Poe Museum, or fawn over the otters at Maymont. Buy everything in Carytown. Then see it all from atop the Pottersfield Bridge.

Espresso at Blanchard’s. Fig pastry at Sub Rosa. Almond biscuit at Sugar & Twine. Greek Salad at Stella’s. Burrata at Lost Letter. Hot honey pizza at Tazza. Softshell crab at the Roosevelt. Lemon danish at Idle Hands. RVA cider at Buskey’s. Pulled pork sandwich at ZZQ. Fresh fruit cake from the Shyndigz 24/7 vending machine.

You can view a map of our favorites here.

Richmond is a bustling hub of amazing food, unique architecture and tight-knit neighborhoods. We can't wait to show you around!

Short Term Rentals : Our wedding will take place in the heart of Richmond's historic Fan District. We highly recommend you consider staying in an Airbnb or Vrbo within walking distance of The Branch Museum! Please let us know if you need any help selecting a great stay.

Linden Row Inn, 100 E. Franklin St. : A cozy inn made up of beautifully restored 19th century row houses, this spot is quintessential Richmond. The enclosed garden was even a childhood haunt of local legend Edgar Allan Poe.

We have reserved a block of rooms for our guests at discounted rates. To book, call 804-783-7000 and ask for the OBrion-Thakkar Wedding Block. You can also book with our group rate here. The group rate will be available until August 11, or until rooms in the block sell out.


Quirk Hotel Richmond, 201 W. Broad St. : Built in 1916, this former department store is known for serving excellent food and drink in stylish surroundings.

The Jefferson Hotel, 101 W. Franklin St. : Join the bride and groom and stay at a Richmond classic known for its grand Beaux-Arts architecture - treat yourself. Sadly, the fountain no longer features live alligators.

The Graduate Hotel, 301 W. Franklin St. : A mid-range option by Hilton with a rooftop bar for fans of collegiate design.

FAQ

When should I RSVP by?

Please RSVP no later than August 10, 2026.

What should I wear to the ceremony & reception at the Branch Museum?

The dress code is Garden Party Cocktail. The event will take place outdoors on a grass lawn and inside the museum. Suits, festive dresses in elevated fabrics, saris and lehengas are all appropriate. Both western and Indian attire are welcome!

Where should I park?

If you plan to drive, you must park in the church lot adjacent to The Branch Museum at 2551 Monument Avenue. Street parking is reserved for area residents only. Cars cannot be left in the lot overnight.

Can I bring someone?

Please stick to only those family members named on your invitation.

What time should I arrive?

We ask that you arrive promptly by 5:15 for a 5:30PM ceremony.

What should I expect at a fusion ceremony?

We are so excited to showcase both of our cultures in our garden ceremony, all guided by our officiant Christine. A detailed explanation of each element will be provided to our guests so that you can follow along. We have a wonderful photographer & video team, so we ask that you put your phones away and remain seated in order to participate fully in this very special moment.

I have a food allergy, can I make a special request?

Yes! Please let us know any dietary restrictions in advance so we can accommodate.

Is there a quiet place at the venue in case I need a break?

Yes, the small library on the upper level of the Branch is available if you need it.
