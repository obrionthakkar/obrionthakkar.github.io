Indian Festive Attire

The dress code for this event is Indian Festive. We would love for you to embrace Devang’s culture by wearing, if you choose, celebratory Indian clothing. This includes kurtas, sarees, lehengas, anarkalis, and palazzo sets in bold prints and bright colors. You can also find many “Indowestern” outfits that blend both cultures.

Please avoid white, black and red. We would be delighted to help you find an outfit, so please reach out with any questions.

Here are some places where you can find Indian clothing:

  • Lashkaraa
  • House of Indya
  • Kynah
  • Utsav Fashion
  • Kalki Fashion
  • Rental options available at Marigold, Borrow the Bazaar, and Pickle.
  • Second-hand options available at Chutney’s Buy/Sell Indian Clothes and Facebook Marketplace.
